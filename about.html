Our Story
Blissful Bitee

A bakery born of patience

Every cake we send out carries a story — of slow rising dough, careful hands, and small moments of joy made tangible. This is ours.

Blissful Bitee began in a small kitchen in Mian Channu with one sourdough starter, two hands, and a stubborn belief that good cake doesn't need a storefront — it needs care. Years later, the kitchen is still small, the hands are still the same, and the cakes still take longer than they should because nothing about them is rushed.

We started baking for friends and family — birthdays, anniversaries, the small celebrations that needed marking. Word travelled. Then strangers started messaging. Then the messages turned into a small, steady business that still feels personal because it is personal. Every cake that leaves this kitchen has been touched by the person who baked it.

We don't keep cakes on a shelf. We don't bake until you order. The morning of your event, we're in the kitchen at four, weighing flour, melting butter, folding cream. By the time you receive your cake, it has existed in the world for fewer hours than it took us to make it.

The name "Blissful Bitee" came from a customer's review — "every bite was bliss." We took it as a name and a standard. If a bite of one of our cakes doesn't make you pause, smile, want another — we haven't done our job.
